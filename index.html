About

A leader who grew up inside the delivery.

Azim joined FocusOn Interiors in its earliest years as a Backend Operator. Procurement, coordination, site handovers — he learned the business where design meets the ground, rose to Head of Operations, and in January 2026 was appointed Chief Executive Officer.

The press describes him as a result-oriented leader with “phenomenal insights into the changing landscape of corporate and industrial design”, known for an entrepreneurial vision and a people-first style. His brief is now national: expanding FocusOn across India while keeping its hallmark — speed, precision and single-point accountability — intact.

  • Head of Operations → Chief Executive Officer, FocusOn Interiors
  • Led delivery for L&T Finance, Luminous, Infosys, Ericsson and more
  • Focus on people, process and performance

How he leads

People. Process. Performance.

The three words the press attached to his appointment — and the operating system of a company that promises design delivered exactly as envisioned.

01

People first

FocusOn hires for attitude over experience and builds long-term partnerships on transparency. Azim’s people-first style is the reason clients such as L&T Finance came back for a second office.

02

Process discipline

Discover → Define → Design → Procure & Execute → Reveal. One ERP across design, procurement, finance and site — so what is visualised is what gets built.

03

Performance on the ground

Speed, precision and cost discipline — the CQET values (Consistency, Quality, Economical, Time-efficiency) that FocusOn has stood on since 2017.
