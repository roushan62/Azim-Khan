Governance

Structure after the merger.

In December 2025 FocusOn Interior Decorators Pvt. Ltd. merged with Riya Enterprises, an NBFC-focused fit-out specialist. Both businesses now operate under a single “FocusOn Interiors” identity.

  • Chairman: Raja Khan — the merged entity “will be driven under the leadership of Mr. Raja Khan as Chairman” (ANI, 17 Dec 2025).
  • Chief Executive Officer: Azim Khan — appointed January 2026 to lead day-to-day operations and national expansion.
  • Founder & Director: Shanu Khan — leads the corporate vertical; CII member.
  • Directors: Nadeem Khan, Atif Khan; Project Director: Sarim Khan.
  • Legal entity: FocusOn Interior Decorators Private Limited, Gurugram, Haryana.
Operating principles

How decisions get made.

Single-point accountability

One partner owns design, procurement and execution — no finger-pointing between vendors.

One system of record

Design, procurement, finance and site teams work inside a single ERP; digital dashboards make progress visible.

Time discipline

Milestone tracking with MS Project / Primavera; delays surfaced early, not at handover.

Transparency

Rate analysis and BOQs shared openly — the basis of long-term client partnerships.
