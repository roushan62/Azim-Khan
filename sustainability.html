CSR & Sustainability

Sustainability without hype.

FocusOn does not label every office “green”. It makes practical choices that make a workplace function responsibly from day one — and it supports a large workforce across India’s construction ecosystem.

Luminous · HaridwarGreen wall for air quality
In practice

Built into the project, not the brochure.

BW Businessworld documented how sustainability shows up in FocusOn’s work — through simple, honest decisions rather than marketing.

  • Luminous, Haridwar: a living green wall installed to improve indoor air quality — “not as a design feature”.
  • L&T Financial Services: the cafeteria moved to an open balcony so employees eat and work in natural light and fresh air, reducing dependence on artificial cooling.
  • Regent Lighting, Noida: desks aligned to natural views and morning light — lower energy use, better focus.
Materials & methods

Choices that make practical sense.

Recycled PET acoustic panels

Acoustic comfort from recycled material — used across meeting rooms and open floors.

Low-VOC paints

Healthier indoor air for the people who spend their days in the space.

Energy-efficient lighting

LED systems and daylight-led planning to cut operating energy.

Daylight & ventilation first

Layouts planned around natural light and airflow before mechanical systems are sized.

Next step, per BW Businessworld: integrating energy simulation and IGBC / LEED tools to measure daylight, HVAC efficiency and material impact at the planning stage.

Social responsibility

Work that creates work.

Beyond 100+ direct professionals, FocusOn supports a large indirect workforce — contractors, site engineers, fabricators and skilled tradespeople — across every city it builds in.

  • Expansion into Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata is expected to generate employment across design, engineering, project management and on-site execution roles.
  • Hiring for attitude over experience opens doors for people early in their careers — the path Azim himself took.
  • During the pandemic FocusOn kept crews working safely with PPE to complete a healthcare project at Sanar Hospital, Gurugram.
