About

Built on the ground, not in the boardroom.

Azim Khan is the Chief Executive Officer of FocusOn Interiors — a PAN-India design-and-build firm headquartered in Gurugram. He is based in New Delhi.

Official press photographFocusOn Interiors
Biography

Eight years, one company, every rung.

“From starting my journey as a Backend Operator to leading FocusOn Interiors as CEO…” — that is how Azim introduces himself on LinkedIn, and it is the most accurate summary of his career.

He joined FocusOn Interiors — founded in Gurugram in 2017 by Raja Khan and Shanu Khan — in its formative years. The back-end is where fit-out businesses are won or lost: rate analysis, BOQs, vendor negotiation, procurement schedules, and the daily coordination between drawings and site. Azim built his career there, moved up to lead operations, and became Head of Operations as the company pivoted from a design studio into a delivery-led firm.

Along the way he worked on projects for organisations including Infosys, L&T Finance and Luminous, and — in the words of Realty+ — is “credited with shaping FocusOn Interiors into a PAN-India solutions provider”. In January 2026, following the company’s merger with Riya Enterprises, he was appointed Chief Executive Officer.

The company

FocusOn Interiors, in brief.

Founded in 2017 as a small fit-out set-up, FocusOn has grown into a 100+ member design-and-build organisation working across corporate offices, NBFCs, industrial facilities, education, healthcare, logistics and retail. It delivers turnkey projects — concept, design development, procurement, execution and styling — under a single point of accountability.

  • Values: CQET — Consistency, Quality, Economical, Time-efficiency
  • Tagline: “Spaces Styled, Stories Told”
  • Tools: AutoCAD, Revit/BIM, SketchUp, 3ds Max, Enscape, R-Dash; VR/AR walkthroughs; one ERP across the business
  • December 2025: merged with Riya Enterprises (NBFC fit-out specialists) under a unified FocusOn identity
