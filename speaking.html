Speaking & Panels

Conversations about how India builds its workplaces.

Azim speaks from eight years on the delivery side of commercial interiors — the part of the industry that rarely gets a microphone.

Topics

What he can speak to.

From design studio to delivery-led firm

How FocusOn repositioned around execution — single-point accountability, ERP-led coordination, and what changed for clients.

Procurement as a competitive edge

Rate analysis, BOQ discipline and vendor partnerships — the back-end craft that decides whether a fit-out lands on time and on budget.

Industrial & NBFC workplaces

Lessons from 125,000 sq ft for L&T Finance and a 45,000 sq ft office inside Luminous’ Haridwar campus.

Technology that solves, not showcases

VR validation, BIM coordination and digital tracking — used to remove rework rather than to impress in the pitch.

Backend Operator to CEO

A career story about growing inside one company, and what it teaches about building teams that hire for attitude over experience.

Scaling PAN-India

Opening new cities and new verticals — QSR, F&B and central kitchens — without losing consistency, quality or speed.
