Vision & Philosophy

Future-ready interiors, delivered exactly as envisioned.

What Azim Khan is building at FocusOn Interiors — and the principles the company has stood on since 2017.

The vision

Three commitments in one sentence.

Azim’s appointment statement contains the whole strategy. Read it slowly and three commitments fall out.

01

Future-ready, innovative interiors

Workplaces designed for hybrid teams and shifting occupancy — validated in VR before construction, coordinated in BIM, and tuned after move-in with occupancy data.

02

Workplaces and industrial environments

FocusOn’s edge is breadth: NBFC headquarters, factory-campus offices such as Luminous Haridwar, tech offices, skilling campuses — and now QSR, F&B and central-kitchen fit-outs.

03

Presence across India

From Gurugram, Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru to Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata — the growth mandate that came with the Riya Enterprises merger.

Philosophy

“Architecture belongs to its users.”

FocusOn works to a belief borrowed from B.V. Doshi — that a building is a living organism that belongs to the people who use it. Every project begins with how people will sit, think, move and behave in a space. Only then do drawings begin.

That is why BW Businessworld singled the firm out for “speed, precision and an almost obsessive attention to what the end user needs” — and why Azim describes his own focus as people, process and performance.

  • P

    People

    Attitude over experience when hiring; transparency and reliability with clients; a people-first leadership style.

  • P

    Process

    Single-point accountability, a shared ERP, and a five-stage method: Discover & Ideate → Define the Vision → Design Development → Procurement & Execution → Styling & Final Reveal.

  • P

    Performance

    CQET — Consistency, Quality, Economical, Time-efficiency — measured on every site, not just promised at the pitch.

How work happens

The FocusOn method.

  1. Discover & Ideate

    Understand the people, the brand and the way the space will actually be used.

  2. Define the Vision

    Brief, budget, timelines and the measurable outcomes the space must deliver.

  3. Design Development

    Plans, 3D and VR walkthroughs, BIM coordination and material evidence.

  4. Procurement & Execution

    Rate analysis, BOQs, vendor negotiation and disciplined site delivery.

  5. Styling & Final Reveal

    Handover, snag closure and post-occupancy learning.
